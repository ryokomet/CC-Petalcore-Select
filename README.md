# Petalcore Select

Plant recommendation site. Suggests plants based on a user's space and preferences.

## How it works

The website collects choices like indoor or outdoor space, light, care level, and plant interests. The backend compares those choices with information in the plant catalog and returns a ranked list. The scores are suggestions based on the catalog, not a promise that a plant will grow well in every situation.

## How to use it

Open Petalcore Platform and choose **Select**. Choose the options that describe your space and the kinds of plants you like, then submit the form to see recommendations. The working recommendation service is part of the Platform deployment.
